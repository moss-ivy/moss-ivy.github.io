About Ivana Dobiášová

Multidisciplinary designer exploring the dialogue between organic living systems, craft, and physical products.

Design Philosophy

My creative work is rooted in harmony with nature. Across diverse design scales—from miniature jewelry and artisanal glassware to industrial consumer products and architectural interventions—I strive to create objects that evoke emotional resonance and long-term utility.

I believe that physical products in our spaces should enrich everyday human rituals. By studying biomimetic patterns and combining modern digital fabrication with timeless physical materials, every piece tells an honest story.

Areas of Expertise

Physical & Product Design

Functional home goods, lighting systems, vessels, and sensory everyday objects.

Industrial & Ergonomic Design

Production-ready engineering concepts, ergonomic furniture, and sustainable materials.

Jewelry & Wearable Art

Precious metals, organic forms, and sculptural wearable pieces.

Visual & Graphic Identity

Brand typography, spatial wayfinding, tactile packaging, and portfolio curation.

Architecture & Spatial Installations

Site-specific installations, biophilic spaces, and interior object integration.
